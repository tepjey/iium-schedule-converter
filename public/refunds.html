Refund Policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

Changed your mind? You can get a full refund within 14 days of buying SlipSnap Pro, no questions asked.

How to ask for a refund

Email [contact email] from the email you paid with, and include the receipt number from your receipt email (it looks like “Receipt #1234-5678”).

After 14 days

Refunds after 14 days are given if Pro doesn’t work for you and we can’t fix it, if you were charged twice, or if a Pro feature you paid for is removed without a replacement.

How long it takes

Refunds go back the way you paid, through Stripe. Card and GrabPay refunds usually arrive within 5 to 10 business days. FPX refunds go to the bank account you paid from, which can take up to 10 business days.

What happens to Pro

Once refunded, Pro can no longer be restored with your receipt, and your Pro key should no longer be used. Images you already saved are yours to keep.
